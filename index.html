I am an Assistant Professor (Lecturer) at ESCI–UPF in Barcelona and Academic Director of the International Business Program. My research lies at the intersection of entrepreneurship, strategy, and organization theory, focusing on how entrepreneurs, firms, and other economic actors respond to social, moral, and sustainability challenges.

Alongside my academic work, I founded GVS Consultoria, a Barcelona-based consultancy supporting entrepreneurs and businesses from immigrant and marginalized communities — direct, ongoing exposure that complements my research and teaching.

Background

I grew up in Sylhet, Bangladesh, and studied at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, where alongside my coursework I co-founded a student association focused on community support and became involved with an arts and social-impact initiative. I moved to Barcelona for postgraduate study, a transition that reshaped how I think about research, teaching, and the practical realities entrepreneurs face.
